## ⚙️ How to Use

### 1. Download a Video or Reel

1. Open the Instagram app or website and **copy the link** of the video/reel you want (tap **⋯** → **Copy link**, or just copy the URL from the browser address bar).
2. Go to **Instarow** and select the **Video** or **Reels** tab.
3. **Paste** the link into the download box — the media is fetched automatically (or press **Download**).
4. Choose your preferred **quality** (up to 1080p) and click **Download**.
5. The file (MP4 with sound) is saved straight to your device.

### 2. Download a Photo

1. Copy the link of the Instagram photo post.
2. Go to **Instarow** and select the **Photo** tab.
3. Paste the link, then click **Download** under the preview.
4. The original JPG is saved to your device.

### 3. Download a Carousel Post

1. Copy the link of a multi-image/video Instagram post.
2. Go to **Instarow** and select the **Carousel** tab.
3. Paste the link — Instarow lists **every item** in the post (Image #1, #2, … and any videos).
4. Download each item individually in original quality.

### 4. Extract Audio Only (MP3)

1. Paste any reel/video link as usual.
2. In the results, click **Download Audio** under the **Audio only (MP3)** section.
3. Instarow converts the audio to **MP3** in your browser and saves it.

## ❓ FAQ

**Is Instarow free?**
Yes — 100% free with unlimited downloads. No premium plans, no subscriptions.

**Do I need to log in?**
No. Instarow never asks for your Instagram credentials. Paste a public link and download.

**Can I download reels with sound?**
Yes. When audio is separate, Instarow merges video + audio automatically in your browser (FFmpeg WebAssembly) so the file plays with full sound.

**Does it work on mobile?**
Yes — any device with a modern browser: Android, iPhone, tablet, or desktop. No app installation.

**Is it legal?**

**Can I download private posts?**
